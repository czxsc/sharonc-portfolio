Background Art Design:
Not super feasible to draw 16 different backgrounds (at minimum, one landing/peaceful zone, 3 dungeons per layer) and we didnt want to use same background for everything, dwindles the wonder and exploring aspect. 
Compromise: Designed 4 variations per zone (one more horizontal layout, one more vertical, one more square, and nexus/landing zone) for each, and then used curves to adjust color scheme of the images. Then for some added some accessorizing features for variety
Display all the backgrounds together, but i dont want them to take up too much space. Either find ways to collapse some of them and expand upon hovering, or make them a smaller grid

Tilesets:
TOok many iterations, very unfamiliar territory for me. 
Many different issues. First tileset too complex, then looked too cartoonish compared to everything else, then pattern just didnt stand out from background.
Had issue with original tilesets look very out of place in different areas due to color schemes. 
Took similar strategy as backgrounds in the end, made a base "rock" block with more neutral color, then created a grass stamp overlay, where we adjusted the curves again to create 4 different variations to fit the different areas
